Statements of Crown Prince Reza Pahlavi

Crown Prince Reza Pahlavi's Conversation with Sky News Arabia

Published October 16, 2024

Through sabotage, terrorism, and extortion, the Islamic Republic regime has driven Iran into isolation and tarnished the standing and respect of Iranians within the international community. The questioning of Iran's national sovereignty over its three islands at a joint meeting of the Persian Gulf Arab states and the European Union is a direct consequence of the Islamic Republic's weakness and cowardice. This anti-national regime represents the greatest threat to Iran's land, waters, and territorial integrity.

"If the European Council were serious about addressing the threat of the Islamic Republic, it would designate the IRGC a terrorist organization and back UN snapback sanctions on the regime. Instead, it insults the Iranian people by supporting perversions of history and colonialist violations of Iranian territorial integrity. Iran's sovereignty over the islands of Greater Tunb, Lesser Tunb, and Abu Musa in the Persian Gulf is not up for debate."
