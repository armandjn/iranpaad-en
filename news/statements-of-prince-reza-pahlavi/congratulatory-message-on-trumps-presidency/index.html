Statements of Crown Prince Reza Pahlavi

Crown Prince Reza Pahlavi's Congratulatory Message on Donald Trump's Presidency

Published November 6, 2024

President Trump, congratulations on your election.

In your first term, you stood boldly with the Iranian people against the Islamic Republic — a regime that threatens not only the Middle East but the American people as well, through terror, instability, and chaos. Now you have the opportunity to make history and, by helping bring an end to this threat once and for all, leave behind a legacy of lasting peace. In that mission, the people of Iran are your best partners for peace.
